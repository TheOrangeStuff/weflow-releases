# WeFlow downloads

WeFlow copies photos from a camera card, checks the copies, tells you when the card is
safe to remove, then adjusts, watermarks and uploads the photos to SmugMug or Flickr.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

| Your computer | File to download |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 13 or later) | `WeFlow-<version>-arm64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `WeFlow-Setup-<version>.exe` |

Intel Macs are not supported.

WeFlow tells you when a newer version is published here.
